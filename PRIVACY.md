# Privacy

Christian's Merge Windows doesn't collect, store or send any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers: everything it does happens in your own browser.

When you click the button, it moves tabs between your windows. It never reads what is on the pages.

Nothing it sees leaves your computer, and none of it is shared with anyone.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/christianjbrown/chrome-extension-merge-windows/issues.
